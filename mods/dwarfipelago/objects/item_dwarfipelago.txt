item_dwarfipelago

[OBJECT:ITEM]

	Archipelago shop goods. These are deliberately "orphan" tools: no entity is
	ever granted [TOOL:...] for them, so no civilization crafts or trades them.
	They only ever exist when the mod DFHack-creates one and places it on the
	Archipelago caravan, so they can never appear in a normal caravan.
	All of them share the AP-logo sprite (see graphics_dwarfipelago.txt); the
	NAME is what tells the goods apart on the trade screen.
